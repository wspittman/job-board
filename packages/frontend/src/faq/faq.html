Frequently Asked Questions

We've collected the answers to the questions we hear most so you can get back to finding your next role.

How To Use

I found a great job! How do I apply?

Click the "Apply" button when you find a job you're interested in, and we'll direct you to the employer's official application page. We never stand between you and the employer - you'll always apply directly through their preferred channel.

How do I save my search and come back later?

Save a bookmark to the page. Every time you change your search, the URL in your browser's address bar automatically updates, allowing the bookmark to save the search. Using the bookmark will re-run your search to show updated jobs.

How do I share a job with a friend?

Click the clipboard (Copy) button next to the Apply button. This will copy a sharable URL to your clipboard. Anyone using the URL will be brought back to that specific job for as long as it is available.

Why am I only seeing 24 jobs?

We intentionally limit each search to 24 matches. Add or update your filters to cut out poor matches. You can also use the Order By filter to change which jobs are prioritized into those 24 slots.

The reason we set this limit is that we can't stand job boards that make you scroll through page after page of poorly matching jobs. You should be able to see the best matches and then move on with your day. The limit encourages you to narrow down your search, and it encourages us to build better search so you can find exactly what you want in those 24 slots.

About the Jobs

Where do you find all these jobs?

We pull job posts directly from employers, accessing their applicant tracking systems (ATS) through official APIs. This way, we ensure high-quality, up-to-date posts while our smart search surfaces the most relevant opportunities for you.

Why do you only have US-based jobs?

Most job seekers search within a single country's hiring market, and the US is the one we know best. Hiring practices, norms, policies, and even job titles can vary a lot from country to country. By limiting the board to US-based jobs, we can make the experience more accurate and useful than if we spread our effort across very different markets.

We include jobs located in the US and remote roles open to people working from the US. The employer can be based anywhere.

How do I know these jobs are still up for grabs?

We check employer listings daily and remove closed roles. A job can close between checks, so the employer's application page is the best place to confirm it's still open. We also take old postings off the board after 90 days, even if the employer is still accepting applications.

Other

What data do you collect about me?

We're committed to a privacy-first job search, but we do collect a minimal amount of data to understand how people use the site, to diagnose issues, and to improve the experience.

We store one random ID in this browser and another in this tab. The tab ID goes away when you close the tab. You can clear the browser ID by clearing this site's data in your browser.

When you open a page or perform a search, your browser sends us the page path and those IDs. They don't identify you by name or follow you across browsers or devices.

Our systems automatically delete all individual data after 90 days.

I'm hiring! Can I post my job here?

We don't accept direct job submissions, but we regularly index US-based job posts from employers who use supported applicant tracking systems (Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby). If your company uses one of these systems and its jobs are missing from the board, email us at contact@betterjobboard.net. Include your ATS and company ID and we'll look into adding your jobs.

Do we really need another job board?

We think so! Traditional job boards prioritize employers' needs over a seamless experience for job seekers. This is why so many of them end up with cluttered interfaces and generic search functions. Better Job Board is different: we put the job seeker front and center. This focus leads to a streamlined, user-first experience with powerful, intuitive search and filtering tools. We're here to make finding the right job faster, simpler, and a whole lot better.

Got an idea to make this better?

We'd love to hear it! Send your thoughts to contact@betterjobboard.net. We read every message and use your feedback to shape our improvements. While we can't reply to every message personally, your input goes a long way in helping us build a better experience for all job seekers.
